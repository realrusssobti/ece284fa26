\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Q4: MNIST Multi-Layer Perceptron}\par
Parameter count and operations per input image
\end{center}

\textbf{Architecture.} In the supplied Code4 GPU notebook, a $28\times28$
grayscale image is flattened to $784$ inputs. The three fully connected
layers are
\[
784\xrightarrow{\texttt{fc1}}512
\xrightarrow{\texttt{fc2}}512
\xrightarrow{\texttt{fc3}}10.
\]
Each layer has a bias for every output neuron. The actual forward method
applies ReLU after \emph{all three layers}, including the final 10 outputs.

\section*{1. Total parameters}
A fully connected layer with $n_{\rm in}$ inputs and $n_{\rm out}$ outputs has
$n_{\rm in}n_{\rm out}$ weights and $n_{\rm out}$ independent bias parameters:
\[
P=n_{\rm in}n_{\rm out}+n_{\rm out}.
\]
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Layer & Weights & Bias parameters & Total parameters\\
\midrule
\texttt{fc1}: $784\to512$ & $784(512)=401{,}408$ & $512$ & $401{,}920$\\
\texttt{fc2}: $512\to512$ & $512(512)=262{,}144$ & $512$ & $262{,}656$\\
\texttt{fc3}: $512\to10$ & $512(10)=5{,}120$ & $10$ & $5{,}130$\\
\midrule
Total & $668{,}672$ & $1{,}034$ & $669{,}706$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\[
\boxed{P_{\rm total}=401{,}920+262{,}656+5{,}130
 =669{,}706\text{ parameters}.}
\]
Flattening and ReLU introduce no trainable parameters.

\section*{2. Total operations per image}
Each output neuron computes a dot product with $n_{\rm in}$ inputs,
giving $n_{\rm in}n_{\rm out}$ MACs per layer. Using the specified rules,
one MAC costs $2$ operations, one bias addition costs $1$, and one ReLU
costs $1$. Therefore, for each layer in this network,
\[
O=\underbrace{2n_{\rm in}n_{\rm out}}_{\text{MAC operations}}
  +\underbrace{n_{\rm out}}_{\text{bias additions}}
  +\underbrace{n_{\rm out}}_{\text{ReLU operations}}.
\]
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Layer & MACs & MAC operations & Bias adds & ReLUs & Total operations\\
\midrule
\texttt{fc1} & $401{,}408$ & $802{,}816$ & $512$ & $512$ & $803{,}840$\\
\texttt{fc2} & $262{,}144$ & $524{,}288$ & $512$ & $512$ & $525{,}312$\\
\texttt{fc3} & $5{,}120$ & $10{,}240$ & $10$ & $10$ & $10{,}260$\\
\midrule
Total & $668{,}672$ & $1{,}337{,}344$ & $1{,}034$ & $1{,}034$ & $1{,}339{,}412$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\[
\boxed{O_{\rm total}=803{,}840+525{,}312+10{,}260
 =1{,}339{,}412\text{ operations per image}.}
\]

\textbf{Counting scope.} The total covers one forward pass on a preprocessed
image. Flattening adds no arithmetic. Training, preprocessing, and device
transfers are excluded; GPU execution does not change this count.
The final \texttt{argmax} has no specified comparison cost. If one comparison
costs one operation, selecting the largest of 10 outputs adds $10-1=9$,
giving $1{,}339{,}421$ operations including class selection.

\textbf{Verification.} Instantiating the notebook's \texttt{Net} class and
summing \texttt{p.numel()} over its parameters gives $669{,}706$.
Layer dimensions and the three ReLU calls give the operation totals above.
